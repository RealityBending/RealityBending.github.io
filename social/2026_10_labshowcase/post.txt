🧠 Research spotlight: the Reality Bending Lab

How does the brain decide what is real, and what happens when it gets it wrong?

The Reality Bending Lab, led by Dominique Makowski here in the School of Psychology, studies how we perceive reality and how that perception gets distorted: illusions, fake news, beliefs about AI-generated content, deception, and how all of these connect with our emotions, cognitive control and sense of self.

To answer these questions, the lab records signals from the body (heart rate, skin conductance…) and the brain (EEG), analyses them with Bayesian and computational models, and builds open-source tools used by researchers around the world.

Illusions, AI art, heartbeats, brain stimulation and a lot of lab dinners. Interested? Find out more at https://realitybendinglab.com
