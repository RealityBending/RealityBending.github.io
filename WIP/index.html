Reality Bending Lab

We study consciousness, perception, and the nature of subjective experience — probing where reality bends and the mind makes it whole.

Research

Our work spans altered states of consciousness, body ownership illusions, and predictive approaches to perception and the self.

People

An interdisciplinary group of researchers at the intersection of cognitive neuroscience, psychology, and philosophy of mind.

Publications
